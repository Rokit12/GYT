How it works

From paperwork to proof

  1. 1Verify

    Registry entry, documents, insurance and surveys are checked and hashed into a digital passport.

  2. 2Track

    Signed onboard GPS, AIS and satellite radar are compared. A position counts only when independent signals agree.

  3. 3Prove

    Every event becomes a hash. Batches are anchored on a blockchain, so anyone can check a record was never altered.

  4. 4Settle

    Berth and charter payments wait in escrow and release only when the yacht's arrival is proven.

          About GYT

          Yacht records you can check, not just trust.

          Global Yacht Tokenization gives every yacht a verified digital passport, follows it with independent tracking signals, and seals each record with a hash anyone can verify.

          Why we exist

          Yacht information is scattered and hard to prove

          Registration, surveys, insurance, maintenance and position data live in different places: PDFs, inboxes, registries and transponder feeds that do not talk to each other. Buyers, guests, marinas and investors end up taking someone's word for it.

          What we do

          One passport, three signals, open proof

          We check a yacht's documents once and hash them into a passport. We follow it with signed onboard GPS, public AIS and satellite radar. Every event becomes a record that is anchored on a public blockchain, so changing it later would show.

          What we believe
          01Proof over promises

          If we say a yacht arrived, you can recompute the proof in your own browser.

          02Hashes, not your files

          Documents stay encrypted off-chain. Only their fingerprints are public.

          03Regulated where it matters

          Ownership units go only through licensed partners, to eligible investors, with checks on every transfer.

          04Open data first

          We build on public AIS, open coastlines and satellite data, and say where every signal comes from.

            Where to start making cash

            Start with streams that need no securities licence. Onboarding fees, marina SaaS, berth commission and the tracking subscription can all be sold in month one. They also build the verified fleet and marina network that the token business depends on.

            Add tokenization through a licensed partner. Plug into an existing regulated issuer or broker (for example a VARA-licensed firm in Dubai, a MiCA-authorised CASP in the EU, or a Reg D / Reg S path in the US) and take a revenue share until you hold your own licence.

            Sell the data last. Once you hold months of verified position and maintenance history, insurers and yacht lenders become the highest-margin customers.

            Unit economics to watch

            CAC per yacht through brokers and management companies, not direct ads. One management company can bring 20–80 yachts.

            Revenue per yacht per year across all nine streams. The model above shows how quickly usage revenue (charter, berth) overtakes one-off fees.

            Marina density. Berth revenue only works once a region has enough partner marinas for a yacht to route between them. Win one corridor first, for example Dubai to Abu Dhabi or the French Riviera.

            This is a planning model, not financial advice. Fractional economic interests are likely securities in most markets; issuance, custody, payments and cross-border transfers need jurisdiction-specific legal review.

            Tracking & security stack

            No single signal is trusted alone. AIS can be switched off or spoofed, so the platform fuses three independent sources and only marks a position "verified" when they agree.

            Signal 1AISSelf-reported identity and position from the vessel transponder
            Signal 2Onboard GPS / IoTSigned telemetry from a tamper-resistant tracker every 30 s
            Signal 3Satellite SAR & opticalIndependent detection that works when AIS is dark
            FusionAgreement scoreDistance and time deltas between the three sources
            ProofMerkle anchorHash of the fused track written on-chain

            About OSIRIS

            OSIRIS (Optical/SAR data and system Integration for Rush Identification of Ship models) began as a European Space Agency GSTP project in March 2016, built by Italy's CNR-ISTI with SisTer and Mapsat. It detects vessels in Sentinel-1, COSMO-SkyMed and optical satellite imagery, estimates type, speed and heading, predicts routes and shows results in a webGIS. An improved version was published in Remote Sensing in January 2024.

            How GYT uses it: OSIRIS is a research platform, not a public API. The practical path is a research partnership with CNR-ISTI for the detection models, plus direct Sentinel-1 imagery from the Copernicus Data Space and commercial SAR for revisit speed. GYT runs the same checks on yachts: is there a hull of the right length where AIS says it is, and if AIS went dark, where did the satellite see it?

            What to tokenize in yachts

            A yacht is not one asset. It is a bundle of rights with different buyers, risks and rules. Ranked by how fast you can launch and how much regulatory weight each carries.

            Who is already doing this

            Shipfinex tokenizes commercial ships into Maritime Asset Tokens backed by SPV shares, with entry from about $1,000. It received in-principle approval from Dubai's VARA for a broker-dealer licence in early 2026. Proof that the SPV + regulated-broker model works for vessels.

            Mustaa focuses on yachts using time-based usage tokens, roughly one million yearly tokens per vessel. Closer to membership than investment.

            The gap GYT fills: neither runs the operating layer around the yacht: verified identity, berth network, fused tracking and tamper-evident history. That layer is useful even for owners who never sell a single unit, which gives GYT revenue before any securities licence.

            Design implications

            Use a permissioned token standard such as ERC-3643 for economic units so that only allowlisted, KYC'd wallets can hold them.

            Keep the official flag registration separate. The token points to the registry entry; it does not replace it.

            Launch in one regulated hub. Dubai combines VARA, heavy marina investment and a large superyacht season. The EU under MiCA is the second market.

            Mid-size first. The 30–40 m segment has the most transactions, which means the most owners who want liquidity and the most charter supply.
